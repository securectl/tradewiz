Stock Trading Bot

Automated stock trading via Alpaca or Webull with market-hours awareness and PDT protection.

Paper Trading Only

The stock bot enforces paper trading mode. Alpaca uses paper=True hardcoded in the client. Never use live credentials.

Broker Setup

Alpaca (Recommended)

  1. Create an Alpaca account
  2. Go to Paper Trading and generate API keys
  3. In the app, go to Stock Trading → Broker
  4. Select Alpaca and enter your API Key and Secret Key
  5. Click Test Connection to verify

Webull

  1. Create a Webull developer account
  2. Generate App Key, App Secret, and note your Account ID
  3. In the app, go to Stock Trading → Broker
  4. Select Webull and enter your credentials
  5. Click Test Connection to verify

Trading Strategies

The stock bot uses the same 7 strategies as the crypto bot, adapted for stock market characteristics:

  1. MACD Crossover — Momentum-based entry on MACD signal cross
  2. EMA Trend — SMA8/EMA20 crossover for trend following
  3. RSI Mean Reversion — Fades extreme RSI with MACD confirmation
  4. Momentum Breakout — High-volume breakout above SMA50
  5. Bollinger Band Reversion — Bounces off BB extremes
  6. Grid Mean Reversion — ATR-based distance from SMA50
  7. Trend Continuation — Pullback to EMA20 in confirmed trends

See Crypto Trading Bot for detailed strategy conditions.

Risk Management

The stock bot has all 7 risk gates from the crypto bot, plus two additional gates:

GateRuleDescription
1–7(Same as crypto bot)Kill switch, enabled, paper-only, daily loss, max position, max open, no duplicates
8PDT RuleBlocks day trades if equity < $25K and 3+ day trades in rolling 5 days
9Market HoursOnly trades during US market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET, weekdays)

Market Hours

The stock bot automatically respects US market hours:

  • Regular hours: 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM Eastern, Monday–Friday
  • Holidays: NYSE holiday calendar observed
  • The bot scans during market hours only; it pauses outside trading windows
  • Check GET /api/stock-bot/market-hours for current market status

Custom Tickers

You can add custom stock tickers beyond the default list. Use the Add Ticker feature in the Stock Trading tab — the app validates the ticker with the broker before adding it.

Position Sizing

Same ATR-based sizing as the crypto bot, with one difference: stock positions are always rounded to whole shares (int(size_usd / price)).
