Trade Tracker

Journal your trades, set weekly goals, and track P&L across your portfolio.

Overview

The Trade Tracker is a personal trading journal that helps you log trades, track performance, and set profit goals. It automatically calculates P&L when you close positions and rolls results into weekly/monthly summaries.

Journal Entries

Creating an Entry

  1. Go to the Tracker tab
  2. Enter the ticker symbol, action (BUY or SELL), quantity, and price
  3. Add optional notes for your reasoning
  4. Click Add Entry

Automatic P&L Calculation

When you add a SELL entry for a ticker you previously bought, the system automatically:

  • Finds the matching BUY entry
  • Calculates the realized P&L
  • Adds the P&L to your weekly goals tracking

Deleting Entries

Deleting a journal entry also reverses any P&L that was recorded from it, keeping your goal tracking accurate.

Weekly Goals

Set a weekly profit target to track your trading performance:

  1. In the Tracker tab, set your weekly target amount (default: $50)
  2. As you close trades, P&L is automatically accumulated toward the weekly goal
  3. View your progress as a percentage with visual progress bar

Account Balance

You can manually update your account balance in the Tracker. This is separate from bot trading balances — it's for tracking your overall portfolio value.

API Endpoints

GET /api/journal login_required
List journal entries, optionally filtered by ticker. Paginated.
POST /api/journal login_required
Add a journal entry. Auto-calculates P&L for SELL actions.
DELETE /api/journal/<id> login_required
Delete a journal entry and reverse P&L if applicable.
GET /api/goals login_required
Get weekly/monthly P&L goals, balances, and progress.
POST /api/goals login_required
Update weekly target amount.
POST /api/goals/balance login_required
Update account balance.
