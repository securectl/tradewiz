Crypto Trading Bot

Automated crypto trading on BloFin with 7 strategies, risk management gates, and LLM validation.

Paper Trading Only

The bot enforces paper trading mode (BLOFIN_DEMO=1) as a safety measure. This is checked at the client level and cannot be bypassed from the UI.

BloFin Setup

  1. Create a BloFin account
  2. Go to API Management and create a new API key
  3. Enable Trade permissions
  4. Copy the API Key, Secret, and Passphrase
  5. In the app, go to Crypto Trading → Platform and enter your credentials
  6. Click Test Connection to verify
Tip

API credentials are encrypted per-user using Fernet encryption and stored in the database. They are never exposed in plain text after saving.

Trading Strategies

Seven strategies are evaluated in order for each coin. The first match wins.

Strategy 1: MACD Crossover

Enters when MACD crosses its signal line with RSI confirmation and price above/below EMA20.

  • BUY: Bullish MACD cross + RSI 30–60 + price > EMA20
  • SELL: Bearish MACD cross + RSI 40–70 + price < EMA20

Strategy 2: EMA Trend (SMA8/EMA20 Cross)

Trades the crossover between the 8-period SMA and 20-period EMA.

  • BUY: SMA8 crosses above EMA20 + RSI 30–60
  • SELL: SMA8 crosses below EMA20 + RSI 40–70

Strategy 3: RSI Mean Reversion

Fades extreme RSI levels when MACD confirms reversal.

  • BUY: RSI < 35 + MACD histogram positive
  • SELL: RSI > 70 + MACD histogram negative + price < SMA8

Strategy 4: Momentum Breakout

Catches strong momentum moves with volume confirmation.

  • BUY: Price > SMA50 + volume surge >1.5x + RSI 55–70 + MACD positive
  • SELL: Price < SMA50 + volume surge >1.5x + RSI 30–45 + MACD negative

Strategy 5: Bollinger Band Reversion

Trades bounces off Bollinger Band extremes.

  • BUY: BB position ≤ 10% + RSI < 40 + MACD histogram positive
  • SELL: BB position ≥ 90% + RSI > 65 + MACD histogram negative

Strategy 6: Grid Mean Reversion (ATR-Based)

ATR-measured distance from SMA50 — catches deep pullbacks in trends.

  • BUY: 2+ ATR below SMA50 + RSI < 40 + uptrend (SMA50 > SMA200)
  • SELL: 2+ ATR above SMA50 + RSI > 65 + downtrend (SMA50 < SMA200)

Strategy 7: Trend Continuation / DCA Re-Entry

Buys pullbacks to EMA20 in confirmed trends.

  • BUY: Confirmed uptrend + price within 0.5% of EMA20 + RSI 40–55 + MACD positive
  • SELL: Confirmed downtrend + price within 0.5% of EMA20 + RSI 50–60 + MACD negative

Risk Management

Circuit Breakers

  • Kill Switch: Emergency stop — closes all positions and halts trading. Auto-triggered when daily loss exceeds the limit.
  • Per-Coin Cooldown: 30 minutes after a profitable trade, 60 minutes after a loss on the same coin.
  • ATR Floor: max(ATR, price × 0.5%) — prevents stop-loss collapsing to zero on low-volatility assets.

Exit Rules

  1. Hard Stop Loss / Take Profit — Triggered when price hits SL or TP levels
  2. Trailing Stop — Activates at +1.5% profit, locks at 50% retracement
  3. Time-Based Exit — Closes trades open > 24 hours with P&L between -0.3% and +0.3%
